# Brag Plan: Abdul Basit — Video Editor Portfolio

## What is this app?
A portfolio site for Abdul Basit, a freelance video editor and motion designer, showing
the creators he edits for and a reel of his long-form and short-form work.

## The angle
He edits for creators with millions of subscribers — but the site is the first time
that's ever been added up. Sum the roster and it's **~8.5 million subscribers** watching
videos he cut. The video leads with that number, then proves it with the actual roster
and the actual work. No claims the site doesn't already make.

Critically: this is a video editor's portfolio. The brag video itself has to be cut well,
or it undercuts the pitch. Punchy, on-beat, ember-lit — the video is the portfolio piece.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black. A single number ticks up fast — `0 → 8,506,730` — then settles and the label
resolves underneath: **subscribers**. Ember glow blooms on the settle, landing on the
1.60s strong beat. No name, no logo yet. Just the number.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The roster arriving card by card on the beat with **real avatars and real counts**:
  Mementoe 3.1M, Tayo Ricci 3M, Jack Gordon 853K, HRVizak 316K.
- A real 9:16 short-form card from the site being **tapped** — thumbnail swaps to a
  playing frame. This is the site's actual click-to-play interaction, and it doubles as
  proof he cuts vertical.
- The delivery stats as a single tight row: 30+ creators · 200+ videos · 24–72h.

## Outro / punchline
His own hero line lands in his own type treatment — "Engage. Entertain." then
**"Elevate."** in the ember gradient — with the live URL under it.

## User flow worth showing
Landing-page site, but it has one real interaction worth simulating:
**scroll to a short-form card → tap the ▶ → it plays.**
That's Scene 4 and it is the centerpiece. It shows the product doing its thing rather
than describing itself.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: the editor's own showreel — fast cuts on beat, ember-lit dark, let the client numbers do the bragging
- Interpretation: High energy through motion and hard-ish cuts, never through yanking
  text off early. Every scene boundary sits on a strong beat. Restraint in colour (one
  accent only), aggression in timing.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 22.62s (11.5 bars @ 122 BPM)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm rhythmic bed that drives the cuts
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM) — chosen
  because its strong cues start at **1.60s**, so the hook lands on a beat instead of in dead air
- Music treatment: start at 0.00, full-ish through the body, fade out over the last ~1.2s of the outro
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read. Scene boundaries target strong cues
  **1.60 / 3.70 / 6.34 / 12.65 / 17.91 / 22.65**. Beat grid is ~0.525s; sequential
  creator cards snap to **every other beat (~1.05s)** so each name/count clears the
  readability floor.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use RMS/bass to let the ember glow and the card
  presence breathe. No waveform, no equalizer bars.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched. Counter ticks, card arrivals, one tap, one soft outro hit.
- Audio-coupled moments: the count-up ticks; four card arrivals on alternating beats;
  the simulated ▶ tap; the gradient settle on "Elevate."
- Restraint rule: one accent colour only, and no SFX on anything the viewer must read —
  sound marks arrivals, never competes with text.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The number — 3.70s (0.00–3.70)
Pure `#070a0f`. A large Space Grotesk counter races `0 → 8,506,730`, easing out hard.
It settles ~1.60s on the strong beat; ember glow blooms behind it. Label **subscribers**
fades in under it and holds ~1.3s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — digits roll, then the label resolves after the number settles.
Audio intent: tension into a confident landing.
Audio-coupled idea: sparse tick during the roll, one soft impact on the settle at 1.60s.
Music: driving bed from 0.00.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Who — 2.64s (3.70–6.34)
Line lands: **"…watch videos I edited."** Beat. Then the identity block, matching the
site header: **ABDUL BASIT** / *Video Editor · Motion Design*.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the line first, name block second on the 5.80 beat.
Audio intent: the reveal, calm confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: soft interface tick on the name block arrival.
Music: continues.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The roster — 6.31s (6.34–12.65)
Four real creator cards arrive one by one, ~1.05s apart (every other beat), stacking
vertically: circular avatar + name + sub count.
**Mementoe 3.1M · Tayo Ricci 3M · Jack Gordon 853K · HRVizak 316K.**
Uses the site's real avatar art and real numbers. All four hold together ~1.2s at the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — card-by-card arrival, each with its own sound.
Audio intent: accumulating weight — each card adds to the brag.
Audio-coupled idea: one card sound per arrival on the alternating beat grid.
Music: continues, cards ride the beat.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The work (tap to play) — 5.26s (12.65–17.91)
A 9:16 short-form card from the site sits centre on the dark ground, ▶ button in its
circular glass treatment. A cursor/tap indicator moves in and **taps the ▶** — the button
scales away and the frame swaps to a playing frame with a progress line running.
Two more vertical cards drift in soft-focus behind to imply volume.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on the play button, then state change to playing.
Audio intent: the satisfying click of the product working.
Audio-coupled idea: UI tap on the press; soft whoosh as the player state swaps.
Music: continues.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Stats and sign-off — 4.74s (17.91–22.65)
Tight stat row snaps in on 17.91: **30+ creators · 200+ videos · 24–72h turnaround**,
holds ~1.4s. Cut to his hero line in his own treatment: "Engage. Entertain." then
**"Elevate."** arriving in the `#c43b2f → #ff6b4a` gradient on the 20.54 beat and holding.
URL `basitportfoliowebsite.vercel.app` sits quietly beneath. Music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: yes — stat row first, then the two-part headline.
Audio intent: land it and get out.
Audio-coupled idea: one restrained impact on "Elevate."; music fade over the final ~1.2s.
Music: fades to silence by 22.65.
Transition mood: final hold

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, driving, business-warm
**Audio summary:** A 114.84 BPM bed carries the whole cut; ticks build the hook, card
sounds mark each creator arriving, one tap sells the interaction, and a single restrained
hit lands "Elevate." before the music fades out.

## REVISION 2 — music and tempo (supersedes the Audio direction above)

The bundled `happy-beats-business-moves` bed was rejected as corny; all five
bundled tracks share that corporate-house character. The reference supplied
was **Palmate — "Feeling Blue" (House)**, a commercial release (Beatport /
Spotify) posted by a promo channel that explicitly disclaims ownership, so it
cannot be used from that source.

Decisions taken:

- **Genre re-read**: the reference is melodic/deep **house**, not trap. Motion
  was retuned to groove rather than aggression.
- **Tempo**: the whole edit is now cut to a **122 BPM** four-on-the-floor grid.
  Every scene boundary sits on a bar line; the roster cards land every 2 beats.
- **Music bed**: none rendered. The video ships SFX-only so a licensed track can
  be laid underneath without re-timing a single cut.
- **Re-snapping**: `index.html` derives every onset from one `BPM` constant.
  Changing that single value re-times the entire edit.

### Motion rework (v2)
- Arrivals are **binary** (`tl.set` 0→1) instead of fades — the main reason v1
  read soft and v2 reads percussive.
- Counter **scales with its value** (0.46→1) sharing the count's ease, so the
  number swells as it climbs, then punches and elastic-settles on bar 1.
- **Distinct entrance per element** (blur-scale slam / side-snap / rise) rather
  than one reused fade helper.
- Motion blur on entrances; per-scene slow push-in acting as a camera.
- The tap now compresses **card + finger together** with an elastic release.
- Brief **RGB split** on "Elevate." that resolves clean.
- Downbeat-only ember bloom (every-beat pulsing read as strobe).
